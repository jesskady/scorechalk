1. **SEO-016: Force HTTPS and send HSTS** (High, S). `http://scorechalk.com/` still returns 200 with no redirect, so there's a duplicate copy of the whole site. To fix it, turn on Cloudflare "Always Use HTTPS" and HSTS, and set `url.protocol = 'https:'` in the www redirect in `worker/index.js`.
2. **SEO-003 + SEO-002 + SEO-007: Fix the home page head in one edit** (High, S). The title is still just "Score Chalk", there's no canonical or og:url, and there's no WebSite/Organization JSON-LD. Add canonicals to the app, tournament and legal pages in the same pass.
3. **SEO-028 + SEO-031 + SEO-021: Holiday game-night pages, starting with Skyjo and Rummikub** (Med, M, time-sensitive). In both cases search turned up only iOS scorers and no web score sheet. About 4 weeks remain before Christmas. The pages need builder rules (round doubling, "winner collects") before they're written.
