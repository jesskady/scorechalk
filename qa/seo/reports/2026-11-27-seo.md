## Part 1: Technical

### Top 3

1. **SEO-040: Stop `?v=` bumps from re-dating every page** (High, S, new).
   - Feature commits that also bump `shared.css?v=` on every page can't carry `Sitemap: chrome`, so today's deploys dated all 28 URLs to today and pinged IndexNow for all of them.
   - Fix: filter by diff content in `contentCommits`, not by the commit message.
2. **SEO-041: Link `/games/` to the guides** (Med, S, new).
   - `/games/` is the second-best-linked page on the site.
   - Its 12 Builder entries all point at `/builder/#…`, which crawlers read as `/builder/`. None of them links to a guide.
3. **SEO-039 and SEO-005: Finish the measurement setup** (Med, partly done).
   - Cloudflare data now flows. Search Console and Bing access for the audit are still missing.
   - After the next deploy, confirm in Bing Webmaster that the IndexNow pings arrive.

### New since last run

- **SEO-040: Ignore `?v=` bumps by content.** In `scripts/pages.mjs`, drop a commit for a page when its diff, with every `?v=N` stripped, is empty. This keeps lastmod and IndexNow honest no matter how a commit is labelled. Bing's July 2025 guidance says it uses lastmod to choose what to recrawl. Impact High, effort S.
- **SEO-041: A "How to score …" link per entry on `/games/`.** This sends the hub page's internal-link weight to the 16 guides instead of collapsing it into `/builder/`. Impact Med, effort S.

## Research covered this run

- **Bing on lastmod and IndexNow (Technical):** Bing's July 2025 post says it uses accurate lastmod to decide what to recrawl and may skip unchanged URLs. IndexNow is the route for individual changes. In 2023, 18% of sites had wrong lastmod values. This fed SEO-040.
- **Rubber bridge score pads (Content & UX):** the field is three iOS apps (one rated 2.3★) plus a family of thin everycalculators.com pages with wrong worked examples. No playable web score pad turned up. This fed SEO-042.
- **Hub-and-spoke internal linking (Technical):** vendor sources agree that a category hub should link down to its cluster pages and receive links back. They're opinion, not search-engine documentation, but they match Google's general advice on crawlable links. Combined with Google ignoring `#fragments`, this fed SEO-041.
