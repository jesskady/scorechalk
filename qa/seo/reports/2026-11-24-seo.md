## 1. Top 3 this week

1. **SEO-016: Force HTTPS and send HSTS** (High, S). `http://scorechalk.com/` still serves every page with 200 and no redirect, which makes a duplicate copy of the whole site. To fix it, turn on Cloudflare "Always Use HTTPS" and HSTS, and set `url.protocol = 'https:'` in the www redirect in `worker/index.js`.
2. **SEO-003 + SEO-002 + SEO-007: Fix the home page head in one edit** (High, S). The title is just "Score Chalk", there's no canonical or og:url, and there's no WebSite/Organization JSON-LD. Do it in one pass on `public/index.html`, and add canonicals to the app, tournament and legal pages.
3. **SEO-028 + SEO-021: Skyjo first, then the holiday game-night pages** (Med, M, time-sensitive). Skyjo has 8+ iOS-only scorer apps and no web scorer that turned up in search. Add the builder's "double the closer's score" rule and a `/skyjo-score-keeper/` page this week. About 4½ weeks remain before Christmas.
