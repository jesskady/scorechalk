## 1. Top 3 this week

1. **SEO-016: Force HTTPS and turn on HSTS.** `http://scorechalk.com/` serves the full site with **200 over plain HTTP**: no redirect and no HSTS. Turn on Cloudflare "Always Use HTTPS" and HSTS, and set `url.protocol = 'https:'` in the Worker's www redirect. It takes 5 minutes, and it removes a duplicate copy of every page.
2. **SEO-004: Set up Search Console (domain property) and Bing Webmaster, and submit the sitemap.** It's still the prerequisite for measuring anything. There's no verification meta live.
3. **SEO-003 + SEO-002: Home title and description, and self-canonicals.** The live home title is still just "Score Chalk", and the home and app pages still have no canonical. Add them, and noindex shared links (SEO-001) in the same change.

Close behind, and time-sensitive: **SEO-021**. Skyjo, Oh Hell and UNO pages need to be live by early December to catch holiday game-night searches.

## 6. Research covered this run

- **Favicon in Google Search:** it's read from the home page, has to be a crawlable stable URL, square, and ≥48px is recommended. Led to SEO-017.
- **llms.txt / AI search:** studies by Ahrefs and SE Ranking found no citation lift and that 97% of files are never fetched, and Google says it isn't used. **No suggestion made.** Bing indexing is the route to ChatGPT search.
- **Redirect codes on Workers static assets:** 307 comes from `html_handling`, and Google treats temporary redirects as weak canonical signals. Led to SEO-018, and the live checks turned up SEO-016.
- **PWA directories:** pwastore.io still takes free submissions, and PWA List is discontinued. Led to SEO-020.
- **Holiday card games:** Skyjo, Five Crowns, UNO, Oh Hell and Rummy 500 dominate the 2026 roundups. Led to SEO-021.
