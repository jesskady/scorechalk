- **Speculation Rules (Technical):** Chrome documents near-zero next-page LCP, and since January 2026 its mobile "moderate" setting triggers on viewport. Google has made no ranking claim. This fed SEO-037.

- **Read-only Search Console access (Technical: Measurement):** a Restricted user can view most data, and a service account is added by its email. This fed SEO-039.
- **Web Hearts scorers (Content & UX):** one browser competitor, Rarepike. This was added as evidence to SEO-011.
