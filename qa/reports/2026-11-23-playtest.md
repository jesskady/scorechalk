# Score Chalk playtest (2026-11-23): all games, Builder, interruptions, phone width

Scope for this run: home page, Darts (twice, once at phone width), Cribbage, Yahtzee (10 players at phone width), Farkle, Canasta, Magic Commander (phone width), the Builder, Privacy and Terms. I ran the interruption tests (refresh, Back, second tab, close and reopen) on Darts, as last time, and also tried Back in Magic.

This run used headless Chromium driven by scripts. It used a persistent browser profile, so saved games carried over between steps the way they would in a real browser. The second-tab test switched tabs with Playwright's "bring to front", which may not behave exactly like a person clicking a tab.

## Summary
Score entry is quick in every game, and every total I checked was right: Darts remaining scores and averages, Cribbage hand counts (including a double run of four), Canasta rounds with a negative score, Farkle running totals and Magic commander damage. Pending games survived refresh, closing the browser and returning from the home page every time. Two problems from last run are fixed: the home page now updates after Back, and the Darts tile now offers Resume. The three biggest issues:
1. **Two tabs on the same Darts game still silently lose a turn** (still present from 2026-10-11).
2. **The Darts win dialog still can't be closed, and the winning turn can't be undone from it.** Reloading the page turns out to be a hidden way out.
3. **Farkle's ending is confusing.** Nothing is shown when a player passes 10,000, the "Final round" label appears a turn late, and the player who passed 10,000 got one more turn before the game ended.

Magic also has no way back to "All games" from the board short of three menu and setup screens.

## Findings

### 1. Two tabs on the same Darts game still silently lose a turn
- **Severity:** Blocker (a score was lost without warning). **Still present** from 2026-10-11 (finding 1).
- **Where:** Darts game screen open in two tabs
- **Steps:**
  1. Open a Darts game from the home card in tab 1.
  2. Open scorechalk.com in tab 2 and click the same card.
  3. Submit Dana 20, 20, 20 in tab 2 (222 → 162).
  4. Return to tab 1, which still shows Dana on 222 to throw, and submit 5, 5, 5.
  5. Reload tab 2.
- **What happened:** Both tabs end with Dana on 207. The 60 turn is gone, and neither tab warned. Tab 1 never picked up tab 2's turn.
- **Expected:** Tab 1 updates when it's brought back, or it warns before saving over a newer version.
- **Suggestion:** Re-read the saved game when a tab regains focus, or refuse to save over a newer version and offer to reload.
- **Screenshot:** Tab 1 showing Dana on 222 with "Submit 0 for Dana" while tab 2 had Dana on 162.

### 2. The Darts win dialog can't be closed, and a wrong winning turn can't be undone from it
- **Severity:** Hang-up. **Still present** from 2026-10-11 (finding 2).
- **Where:** Darts, end of game
- **Steps:** Check out a player.
- **What happened:** "Mike wins! 301 down in 3 turns · 100.3 average" offers only Rematch and New setup. Escape and clicking outside do nothing, and Undo turn, "All games" and the board stay covered. New this run: reloading the page (or opening the game's address) shows the final board without the dialog, and "Undo turn" then works and the game returns to the home strip. A regular user won't find that. Cribbage ("Undo the last score"), Farkle, Canasta and the Builder ("Back to the scores") all have a way out.
- **Expected:** A "Back to the board" or Undo choice in the dialog.
- **Suggestion:** Add "Back to the board" and "Undo the last turn" to the Darts win dialog to match the other games.
- **Screenshot:** The Darts win dialog with only Rematch and New setup.

### 3. Farkle's ending: no warning when someone passes 10,000, the "Final round" label comes late, and that player got another turn
- **Severity:** Hang-up
- **Where:** Farkle (opened from the home tile), Board view
- **Steps:**
  1. Two players, Hana first and Joel second.
  2. In round 7, give Joel enough to reach 10,350.
  3. Watch the header and whose turn it is.
- **What happened:** When Joel passed 10,000, nothing announced it. On Hana's next turn the header still said "ROUND 7" (the history called it round 8). Only after Hana scored did the header change to "FINAL ROUND", and then it was Joel's turn again. Joel scored 100 more, and only then "Joel wins!" appeared. Under the usual Farkle rule, everyone else gets one last turn and the player who passed 10,000 doesn't throw again. With round-based play, the game would have ended at the end of round 7, since Joel was last in the order. Either way Joel shouldn't have had round 8, and if Hana had passed him on her last turn, Joel's extra turn could have changed the winner.
- **Expected:** When a player reaches 10,000, show something like "Joel reached 10,000. Last turn for everyone else", label that turn as the final round, and end the game after it.
- **Suggestion:** Announce the final round the moment the target is reached, and skip the player who reached it.
- **Screenshot:** The Farkle board on Hana's last turn showing "ROUND 7" with Joel on 10,350 and no message, then the board showing "FINAL ROUND" on Joel's turn.

## What worked well
- The arithmetic was right everywhere I checked: Darts remaining scores, busts, checkout hints (including "D1") and averages; Cribbage hand scoring from cards (fifteens, three of a kind, double run of four, the nobs question); Canasta totals with a negative round and an edited round; Farkle running totals; Magic commander damage also taking life.
