# Score Chalk playtest (2026-10-11): home, Darts, Yahtzee, interruptions

Scope for this run: first impression of the home page, Darts, Yahtzee, and the interruption tests (refresh, back, new tab, close tab), run on Darts.

## Summary
Score entry is quick and the arithmetic was right everywhere I checked. Saving within a single tab held up through refresh, Back, and closing and reopening. The three biggest issues:
1. **Two tabs on the same game silently lose scores.** A turn entered in a stale tab overwrote a turn already entered in the other tab.
2. **The Darts win dialog traps you.** It can't be closed, and the winning turn can't be undone.
3. **After the Back button, the home page's "Pick up where you left off" list is out of date.** It hid the game I had just left and showed an older one.

Darts and Yahtzee also handle resume, undo and the end of the game differently from each other.

I ran the interruption tests on **Darts**. Each game gets its own address (`?g=...`) and play is turn-based, so it was easy to tell whether a turn survived.

## Findings (most severe first)

### 1. A second tab on the same game silently overwrites turns
- **Severity:** Blocker (a score was lost without warning)
- **Where:** Darts game screen open in two tabs
- **Steps:**
  1. Open a Darts game in tab 1.
  2. Open scorechalk.com in tab 2 and click the same game card.
  3. Submit a turn in tab 2 (Dana 60, 222 → 162).
  4. Go back to tab 1, which still shows Dana on 222, and submit a turn there (15).
  5. Reload tab 2.
- **What happened:** Both tabs show Dana on 207. The 60 turn is gone, and neither tab gave any warning. Tab 1 never picked up tab 2's change.
- **Expected:** Tab 1 picks up the change, or warns "this game was changed in another tab" before saving over it.
- **Suggestion:** Reload the game when the tab regains focus, or refuse to save over a newer version and offer to reload instead.
- **Screenshot:** Tab 1 showing "Dana 222, Submit 0 for Dana" next to tab 2 showing "Dana 162, Submit 0 for Mike". Not saved.

### 2. The Darts win dialog can't be closed, and a wrong winning turn can't be undone
- **Severity:** Hang-up
- **Where:** Darts, end of game
- **Steps:** Check out a player.
- **What happened:** The "Mike wins!" dialog only offers Rematch or New setup. Escape and clicking outside do nothing, so "All games", "Undo turn" and the final board can't be reached. If the checkout was entered wrong, it can't be fixed. Yahtzee's version has "Back to the sheet", and Undo still works there after the game ends.
- **Expected:** A way to close the dialog or go back to the board, and Undo on the last turn.
- **Suggestion:** Add "Back to the board" (as Yahtzee does), or make the dialog dismissable.
- **Screenshot:** The "Mike wins! 301 down in 4 turns · 75.3 average" dialog with Rematch and New setup only. Saved to `screenshot-1791749395946-4.jpg`

### 3. After pressing Back, the home page shows an old list of pending games
- **Severity:** Hang-up (it looks as if the game was lost)
- **Where:** Home page, "Pick up where you left off"
- **Steps:**
  1. Start a Darts game and play two turns.
  2. Press the browser Back button.
- **What happened:** The home page opened scrolled to the bottom, so the strip was off screen. Scrolled up, the strip didn't include the current game, only the older Darts and Cribbage games. A manual reload made "Mike 76 · Dana 222" appear. No data was actually lost.
- **Expected:** The game I just left is listed first.
- **Suggestion:** Refresh the strip whenever the home page is shown, including when the browser returns to it with Back.
- **Screenshot:** The home page after Back, showing only "Darts: Player 1 383 · Player 2 397" and "Cribbage". Saved to `screenshot-1791749289020-1.jpg`

### 4. The Darts tile doesn't offer to resume, but Yahtzee does
- **Severity:** Friction
- **Where:** Darts setup screen (`/darts/`)
- **Steps:** With a Darts game pending, click the main Darts tile.
- **What happened:** A blank setup form appeared with no hint that a game was pending. The Yahtzee tile shows "Resume saved game" with the names filled in.
- **Expected:** The same resume option on every game.
- **Suggestion:** Add "Resume saved game" to Darts, and list the pending games if there's more than one.

### 5. "Resume saved game" is a small secondary link under a big "Start Game" button
- **Severity:** Friction
- **Where:** Yahtzee setup screen when a game is pending
- **What happened:** The prominent button starts a new game. Resume is a faint link underneath. A returning player is likely to hit Start Game, and I didn't test whether that replaces the saved game.
- **Suggestion:** Make Resume the main button when a saved game exists, or say what Start Game will do to the saved one.

### 6. You can score in another player's column during someone else's turn without a warning
- **Severity:** Friction
- **Where:** Yahtzee sheet
- **Steps:** During Rosa's turn, tap an empty cell in Theo's column and pick a value.
- **What happened:** It was accepted. The only clue is that the popup says "for Theo" instead of "Theo's turn". On a phone, tapping the wrong column by one is easy.
- **Suggestion:** Show a small "Not Theo's turn — score anyway?" note, or highlight the difference more clearly.

### 7. Undo asks for confirmation in Darts but not in Yahtzee
- **Severity:** Friction
- **Where:** Darts "Undo turn" compared with Yahtzee "Undo"
- **What happened:** Darts shows an "Are you sure?" dialog that explains what will be rolled back. Yahtzee undoes immediately and shows "Undid Theo's Aces" in the header. Both work, but they behave differently.
- **Suggestion:** Pick one behavior. Undo right away with the toast is probably better for both, since undo is itself reversible in spirit.

### 8. Pending-game cards look the same for different games
- **Severity:** Friction
- **Where:** Home page strip
- **What happened:**
  - Yahtzee cards say only "Three players · Rosa's turn", with no names or scores. A blank rematch and a game halfway through look identical.
  - Darts cards show scores but not the starting score or when the game was played.
  - Old cards from earlier sessions ("Player 1 383 · Player 2 397") stay there with nothing to say how old they are.
- **Suggestion:** Show player names and either the leader or the round on Yahtzee cards, and add "last played" times. Consider not listing a rematch until its first score is entered.

### 9. "New setup" after a Darts game forgets the names and settings
- **Severity:** Friction
- **Where:** Darts win dialog, "New setup"
- **What happened:** The players went back to "Player 1"/"Player 2" and the starting score back to 501, even though I'd just played Mike vs Dana at 301. Yahtzee's setup screen fills in the last names.
- **Suggestion:** Fill in the last players and settings.

### 10. Darts gives no way to see or correct past turns
- **Severity:** Friction
- **Where:** Darts game screen
- **What happened:** There's no turn history. Only the last turn can be undone, and fixing it means re-entering all three darts. A mistake from two turns back can only be fixed by undoing every turn since then.
- **Suggestion:** Add a collapsible list of turns, with a tap to edit a turn.

### 11. Darts has two bust controls
- **Severity:** Polish
- **Where:** Darts game screen
- **What happened:** The game detects an overshoot automatically ("Submit 120 — bust"), and there's also a separate red "Bust" button next to "Undo turn". It isn't clear when you'd need the manual button.
- **Suggestion:** Explain what the Bust button is for (for example, darts nobody recorded), or move it away from Undo.

### 12. The Chance total field didn't always get keyboard focus
- **Severity:** Polish (seen once)
- **Where:** Yahtzee Chance popup when it opened above the cell
- **What happened:** Typing right after the popup opened did nothing, so I had to click the field. The 3 of a kind popup earlier did focus the field automatically.
- **Suggestion:** Always focus the number field when these popups open.

### 13. The "You are" choice isn't explained, and it differs between games
- **Severity:** Polish
- **Where:** Darts and Yahtzee setup
- **What happened:** It isn't clear what choosing "You are" does. Yahtzee offers "No one" and Darts doesn't. Yahtzee's win dialog said "Saved to your profile", which suggests that's what it's for, but the setup screen doesn't say so.
- **Suggestion:** Add a one-line explanation and offer "No one" in every game.

## Cross-game consistency
- Resume from the game's own tile: Yahtzee has it, Darts doesn't.
- Undo: Darts asks for confirmation, Yahtzee doesn't. Yahtzee also lets you tap any cell to edit it, while Darts only lets you undo the last turn.
- The control bar is at the bottom in Darts (Undo turn, Bust, Share, New game) and at the top in Yahtzee (Undo, Share, New game).
- Win dialog: Yahtzee has standings, "Back to the sheet", "Saved to your profile" and Undo after the end. Darts has none of these.
- "New setup": Yahtzee fills in the last names, Darts doesn't.
- "You are": Yahtzee includes "No one", Darts doesn't.
- Pending cards: Darts shows scores, Yahtzee only shows the player count and whose turn it is.

## What worked well
- All the arithmetic was correct: remaining scores, averages, bust detection, Yahtzee upper and bonus totals ("N to go"), and the lower and grand totals.
- Refresh and close-and-reopen restored the game exactly, including darts that hadn't been submitted yet.
- The Darts keypad: the multiplier goes back to Single after each dart, the "301 → 216" preview, "Submit 76 — checkout!", and checkout suggestions such as "T20 16".
- The Yahtzee popups: quick-pick values, fixed-score buttons, Scratch, "Empty this box", and a header line that confirms the last action ("Sam: Chance 23").
- Finished Darts games are removed from the pending list on their own.
