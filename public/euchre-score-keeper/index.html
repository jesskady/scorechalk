How euchre scoring works

Euchre is played by two partnerships, partners sitting across from each other. Each hand is five tricks. The side that names trump, the makers, needs at least three of them. The first side to 10 points wins.

What happenedPoints
Makers take 3 or 4 tricks1 to the makers
Makers take all 5 tricks (a march)2 to the makers
A player going alone takes all 5 tricks4 to the makers
A player going alone takes 3 or 4 tricks1 to the makers
Makers take fewer than 3 tricks (euchred)2 to the other side

Going alone, a loner, means the maker's partner sits the hand out, for the chance of 4 points.

These are the common rules. Some tables play to 5, 7 or 11, or give 4 points for euchring a player who goes alone; the score buttons and target can be changed to the way your table plays.

Questions

How many points to win euchre?

10 in the usual game. Some tables play to 5, 7 or 11.

What is a march in euchre?

Taking all five tricks. The side that called trump scores 2 points for it, instead of 1.

How many points for a loner in euchre?

4 if the player going alone takes all five tricks; 1 if they take three or four.

What happens when you get euchred?

If the side that called trump takes fewer than three tricks, the other side scores 2 points.
