Set it up the way your game plays

  • How it ends: first to a target, lowest score wins, a fixed number of rounds, out at zero, or just keep score until you're done.
  • Which way it scores: counting up from zero, or down from a starting total.
  • Scoring buttons: plain numbers — 1, 5, 10 — or named ones like "Natural canasta 500", plus typing any score.
  • Turns and teams: take turns in order or score anyone any time; play as individuals or as partnerships.

Ready-made versions are there for Canasta and Farkle, and any game you set up can be saved to use again.

Keep score on paper, or on a board

Every game taken in turns opens as a scoresheet — a row a round, a column a player, as on paper — or switch to a board of big running totals. Any score can be changed later if it went in wrong, and the change is noted.

Questions

What games can I keep score for?

Any game scored with points: card games like Hearts, Spades, Euchre, Rummy or Phase 10; dice games; board games; trivia nights.

Does everyone need the app?

No app to install — it runs in the browser. Share a link and others join from their own phones, or one phone keeps score for everyone.

Is it free?

Yes, free with no ads and no account needed.
