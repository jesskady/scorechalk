How Rummy 500 scoring works

When a player goes out, the hand ends and everyone scores. Each player adds up the cards they melded or laid off during the hand, and takes away the cards still in their hand. A hand's score can be below zero, and so can a running total. The first player to reach 500 points wins.

An example hand

Jo goes out, having melded three Kings and a run of 5-6-7: 30 + 18 = 48. Sam melded three 9s (27) but is left holding an Ace and a Queen (25): 27 − 25 = 2. Alex melded nothing and is caught with 8, 4 and 3: −15.

Tables differ on Aces and Jokers: some count every Ace as 15, and some play with Jokers as wild cards worth 15. If two players pass 500 in the same hand, the higher total wins.

Questions

How do you score Rummy 500?

At the end of each hand, each player adds up the cards they melded or laid off and takes away the cards left in their hand. Aces are 15, tens and face cards 10, and 2 to 9 at face value.

How many points do you need to win Rummy 500?

500. The first player to reach 500 points wins.

Can your score go negative in Rummy 500?

Yes. If the cards left in your hand are worth more than what you melded, the hand scores below zero, and your total can drop below zero too.

Can I change the scoring to our house rules?

Yes: the Rummy 500 score keeper is built in the Builder, so its target and buttons can be changed and saved.
