How 501 darts scoring works

Each player starts on 501 (or 301, 701…) and takes turns of three darts. The total of each turn is subtracted from the score, and the first to reach exactly zero wins.

The most a turn can score is 180: three treble 20s.

Finishing: double out and busts

In the standard game you must finish on a double — the last dart lands in the double ring, or the bullseye, bringing the score to exactly zero. If a turn would take you below zero, to exactly 1, or to zero without a double, it's a bust: the turn scores nothing and your score goes back to what it was.

Some games also play double in: your score doesn't start going down until you hit a double.

Checkouts

A checkout is the combination that finishes from your score — 170 (treble 20, treble 20, bullseye) is the highest. From 40 the classic finish is double 20.

Questions

What does "double out" mean in darts?

You must finish the game by hitting a double — the outer ring of a number, or the bullseye — that brings your score to exactly zero.

What is a bust in darts?

A turn that would take your score below zero, to 1, or to zero without a double. The turn scores nothing and the score goes back to where it was before the turn.

What is the highest checkout in darts?

170: treble 20, treble 20 and the bullseye.

Can it do 301 or practice on my own?

Yes: 501, 301, 701 or any start, double in and double out on or off, and solo practice.
