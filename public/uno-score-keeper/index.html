How UNO scoring works

A hand of UNO ends when one player plays their last card. That player scores the value of every card still held by the other players; nobody else scores for that hand. The first player to reach 500 points wins the game.

An example hand

Sam goes out. Alex is left holding a 7, a Skip and a Wild; Jo has a 2 and a Draw Two. Sam scores 7 + 20 + 50 + 2 + 20 = 99.

Some tables score the other way round: each player keeps a running total of the cards left in their own hand, and when someone passes 500, the lowest total wins. Newer decks also have special wilds, often worth 40. The score buttons can be changed to the way your table plays.

Questions

How many points do you need to win UNO?

500. The first player to reach 500 points wins the game.

How do you score a hand of UNO?

The player who goes out scores the cards left in everyone else's hands: number cards at face value, Draw Two, Reverse and Skip 20 each, and Wild and Wild Draw Four 50 each.

What are wild cards worth in UNO?

50 points each, for both the Wild and the Wild Draw Four.

Can I change the scoring to our house rules?

Yes: the UNO score keeper is built in the Builder, so its buttons and target can be changed and saved.
