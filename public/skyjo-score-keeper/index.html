How Skyjo scoring works

Every player has twelve cards in a grid, worth −2 to 12. When a player turns over their last card, everyone else has one more turn and the round ends. Each player then adds up their cards (a column of three matching cards is thrown out and counts nothing), and that is their score for the round. Points are bad: when a player reaches 100 at the end of a round, the game ends and the lowest total wins.

The player who ends the round

The player who ended the round has to have the lowest score for it. If anyone else scored the same or less, the ender's score for the round is doubled. A round of zero or less is never doubled.

Questions

How do you score Skyjo?

At the end of each round every player adds up their cards, from −2 to 12, leaving out any column of three matching cards. Points are bad, and the lowest total wins.

What happens if the player who ends the round doesn't have the lowest score?

Their score for the round is doubled, unless it is zero or less. If another player tied them, it is still doubled.

When does Skyjo end?

At the end of the round in which any player reaches 100 points. The player with the lowest total wins.

What are the Skyjo card values?

Cards run from −2 to 12: five −2s, ten −1s, fifteen 0s and ten of each from 1 to 12.

Can I change the limit?

Yes: the Skyjo score keeper is built in the Builder, so the 100 limit can be changed and saved.
