How canasta scoring works

Canasta is usually played by two partnerships to 5,000 points. At the end of each hand, each side adds its bonuses to the value of the cards it melded, and takes off the value of any cards left in hand.

Bonuses

BonusPoints
Natural (clean) canasta: seven cards, no wild cards500
Mixed (dirty) canasta: seven cards with wild cards300
Each red three100
All four red threes800
Going out100
Going out concealed (all at once, no earlier melds)200, in place of 100

Red threes count against a side that has made no meld by the end of the hand.

Card values

CardPoints
Joker50
Two, Ace20
King down to Eight10
Seven down to Four, and black Threes5

The minimum first meld

Side's scoreFirst meld must total
Below 0any amount

These are the common rules; tables vary on some bonuses, and you can change the scoring buttons to your house rules.

Questions

How many points to win canasta?

5,000 in the usual partnership game.

How much is a natural canasta worth?

500 points; a mixed canasta, with wild cards in it, is 300.

What are red threes worth in canasta?

100 each, or 800 for all four — counted against your side if it has made no meld.

Can I change the scoring to our house rules?

Yes: the canasta scorekeeper is built in the Builder, so its buttons and target can be changed and saved.
