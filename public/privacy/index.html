Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Score Chalk keeps score for card, dice, board and pub games. It is built to need as little about you as possible: you can use every game without an account, and nothing is sold, advertised against, or tracked.

Without an account

The games you play are kept in your browser, on your device: the player names you type, the scores, and your settings. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

When you share a game

Tapping Share sends that game — its player names and scores — to our servers, so the links to it work. Anyone holding a link can see the game, and the links to join or keep score can change it, so share them only with the people you mean to. A shared game is deleted when whoever shared it stops sharing it or discards it, and in any case once it has gone 30 days without a change.

When you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, Google tells us your name, email address, profile picture and an account number that identifies you to us; we keep these to make your profile. Games you finish or play while signed in are saved to your profile — player names, scores and turns — along with any scorekeepers you build and save to My games, so you can see your history and carry on from another device. We don't receive your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

Cookies

When you sign in we set one cookie to keep you signed in, and a short-lived one during the sign-in itself. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. Without signing in, Score Chalk sets no cookies at all.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database. Like any web host it handles each request, which includes your IP address.
  • Google provides sign-in, if you use it, and the site's fonts: your browser fetches the fonts from Google, which sees your IP address when it does.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

Your choices

  • Delete any game from your profile, on the profile page.
  • Stop sharing a game from its Share sheet, which ends every link to it.
  • To delete your account and everything saved with it, email hello@scorechalk.com from the address you sign in with, and we will do it within 30 days.

Children

Score Chalk is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep information about them. If you think a child has signed in, tell us and we will delete the account.
