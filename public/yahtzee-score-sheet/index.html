How Yahtzee scoring works

Each turn you roll five dice up to three times, then score the result in one empty box. Every box is filled once — if nothing fits, you score 0 in a box of your choice ("scratch" it). After 13 turns the sheet is full and the highest total wins.

Questions

How do you get the Yahtzee upper bonus?

Score 63 or more across Aces to Sixes — three of each number makes exactly 63 — and you get a 35-point bonus.

What happens if you roll a second Yahtzee?

If your Yahtzee box already has 50, each extra Yahtzee earns a 100-point bonus, and you also score it in another box.

What is the highest possible Yahtzee score?

1,575 — with every box at its best and twelve Yahtzee bonuses — though real games rarely pass 400.

How many players can it handle?

From one to ten, each with their own column.
