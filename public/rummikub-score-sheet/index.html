How Rummikub scoring works

A round of Rummikub ends when a player lays down their last tile. Everyone else adds up the tiles left on their rack, each tile at its number and a joker at 30, and loses that many points. The player who went out scores the total of everyone else's racks, so a round's scores always add up to zero. Play as many rounds as you agree, and the highest total wins.

Questions

How do you score Rummikub?

When a player goes out, every other player loses the total of the tiles left on their rack, and the player who went out scores the sum of all of them.

How much is a joker worth in Rummikub?

A joker left on a rack counts 30 points against the player holding it.

How do you win Rummikub?

Each round is won by going out first. Over several rounds, the player with the highest total wins.

Can I play a set number of rounds?

Yes: the number of rounds can be fixed in the Builder, or play on and tap Finish when you stop.
