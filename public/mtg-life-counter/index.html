How life totals work in Magic

FormatStarting life
Standard, Modern and most constructed formats20
Commander (EDH)40

A player loses when their life reaches 0 or less. Two other counts can also knock a player out:

  • Poison: a player with 10 or more poison counters loses.
  • Commander damage (Commander only): a player dealt 21 or more combat damage by a single commander over the game loses — tracked separately for each opposing commander.
