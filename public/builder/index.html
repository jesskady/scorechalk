    How it ends
    Quick-score buttons Comma-separated, and a name before a number labels the button: Red three 100, Going out 100. Each tap adds that much to the score being entered.
    Counters Kept beside the score, and changed at any time with − and + without taking a turn: a gear bonus, poison, lives. A counter can win the game or put a player out when it reaches a number.
