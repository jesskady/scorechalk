How Farkle scoring works

On your turn you roll six dice and set aside scoring dice, then choose to bank your points or roll the rest. Roll nothing that scores — a farkle — and you lose the points from that turn. The first to 10,000 wins, once everyone has had a final turn.

Questions

How many points do you need to win Farkle?

10,000 is the usual target.

What is a farkle?

A roll with no scoring dice at all. The player loses every point from that turn, and play passes on.

How much are three pairs worth in Farkle?

Commonly 1,500, though some tables score it as 750 — it's one of Farkle's many house rules.

Can I use our own scoring rules?

Yes: the Farkle scorekeeper is built in the Builder, so its buttons and target can be changed and saved.
