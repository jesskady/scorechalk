How cribbage scoring works

A game of cribbage is played to 121 points (or 61 for a short game). Points come at three moments: during the play, when the hands are counted, and once a deal if the starter card is a jack.

The play (pegging)

Players lay cards in turn, keeping a running total that can't go past 31. You score as you play:

Scoring playPoints
Bringing the total to exactly 152
A pair (same rank as the card before)2
Three of a kind ("pair royal")6
Four of a kind ("double pair royal")12
A run of three or more, in any order1 per card
Bringing the total to exactly 312
Go, or the last card of the play (under 31)1

His heels: if the starter card turned up is a jack, the dealer scores 2 straight away.

Counting the hands

After the play, each hand is counted with the starter card as a fifth card: first the player to the dealer's left, the dealer last, and then the dealer's crib.

In the handPoints
Each combination of cards adding to 152
Each pair2
Three of a kind6
Four of a kind12
Each run of three or more1 per card
Flush: four cards of one suit in the hand4 (5 with the starter)
Flush in the crib: all five cards one suit5
His nobs: the jack of the starter's suit1

Runs with pairs count every run: 3-4-4-5 is two runs of three plus a pair, for 8. The highest hand possible is 29; a hand of nothing is jokingly called a "nineteen", since no hand can actually score 19.

Skunks: winning while the loser is still short of 91 is a skunk; short of 61, a double skunk.

Questions

How many points is a cribbage game?

121 points for a standard game, or 61 for a short one.

Who counts first in cribbage?

The player to the dealer's left counts first and the dealer last; the dealer then counts the crib. Being first to count can win the game before the others count at all.

Can it count my hand for me?

Yes. Enter the five cards — the four in your hand and the starter — and it works out the fifteens, pairs, runs, flush and nobs.

Does it work for three players?

Yes, two or three players.
