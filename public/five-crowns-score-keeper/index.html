How Five Crowns scoring works

Five Crowns is played over eleven rounds. The first round deals 3 cards and threes are wild; each round deals one more card and moves the wild rank up, to 13 cards with Kings wild in the last. When a player goes out, everyone else has one more turn, then scores the cards left in their hand. Going out scores 0. After the eleventh round, the lowest total wins.

Questions

How do you score Five Crowns?

When a round ends, each player scores the cards left in their hand: 3 to 10 at face value, Jacks 11, Queens 12, Kings 13, that round's wild cards 20 and Jokers 50. Going out scores 0. The lowest total after eleven rounds wins.

How many rounds are in Five Crowns?

Eleven: from 3 cards with threes wild, up to 13 cards with Kings wild.

How much is a Joker worth in Five Crowns?

50 points, if it is left in your hand when the round ends.

Can I change the scoring to our house rules?

Yes: the Five Crowns score keeper is built in the Builder, so its rounds and buttons can be changed and saved.
