How Phase 10 scoring works

Each player works through ten phases in order, one hand at a time. A player who completes their phase during a hand moves on to the next phase; anyone who doesn't tries the same phase again. When a player goes out, everyone else scores the cards left in their hand, and points are bad. The first player to complete Phase 10 wins. If two players complete it in the same hand, the one with the lower score wins.

Questions

How do you score Phase 10?

When a hand ends, each player scores the cards left in their hand: 5 for each 1 to 9, 10 for each 10 to 12, 15 for a Skip and 25 for a Wild. Points are bad; the player who went out scores 0.

Who wins Phase 10?

The first player to complete Phase 10. If more than one completes it in the same hand, the one with the lowest score wins.

What are the 10 phases in Phase 10?

1: 2 sets of 3. 2: a set of 3 and a run of 4. 3: a set of 4 and a run of 4. 4: a run of 7. 5: a run of 8. 6: a run of 9. 7: 2 sets of 4. 8: 7 cards of one color. 9: a set of 5 and a set of 2. 10: a set of 5 and a set of 3.

Can I change the scoring to our house rules?

Yes: the Phase 10 score keeper is built in the Builder, so its buttons and counter can be changed and saved.
