How Qwirkle scoring works

On your turn you lay tiles in one line, all sharing a colour or a shape. You score one point for every tile in each line you make or add to, counting the tiles that were already in that line, not just yours. A tile you lay can touch two lines at once, across and down, and then it scores in both.

A line can hold at most six tiles: the six colours of one shape, or the six shapes of one colour. Completing one is a Qwirkle, worth 12: 6 for the line and a 6-point bonus. A turn spent trading tiles scores nothing.

When the bag is empty, the first player to lay their last tile ends the game and scores 6 more. The highest total wins.

Two example turns

Kim lays two tiles at the end of a line of two, making a line of four: 4 points. One of her tiles also sits beside a tile on the board in the other direction, making a line of two: 2 more. Her turn scores 6.

Alex lays the sixth tile of a line: a Qwirkle, 12. That tile also makes a line of three the other way: 3 more, 15 in all. On Score Chalk that's Qwirkle and 1, 1, 1, or Qwirkle and a typed 3.

Questions

How do you score Qwirkle?

Each turn scores one point for every tile in each line the turn makes or adds to, counting the tiles already in that line. A tile that is part of two lines scores in both.

How many points is a Qwirkle?

12: a line of six tiles scores 6, and completing it earns a 6-point bonus on top.

How does Qwirkle end?

Once the bag is empty, the first player to use all their tiles ends the game and scores a 6-point bonus. The highest total wins.

What do you score for trading tiles?

Nothing: a turn spent trading tiles back into the bag scores 0.
