How Hearts scoring works

In Hearts, points are bad. At the end of each hand, every player counts the penalty cards in the tricks they took: 1 point for each heart and 13 points for the Queen of spades, 26 in all. When any player reaches 100 points, the game ends and the player with the lowest score wins.

Shooting the moon

A player who takes all thirteen hearts and the Queen of spades has shot the moon: they score 0 for the hand, and every other player takes 26.

An example hand

Alex took 4 hearts, Jo took 6 hearts and the Queen of spades, Sam took 3 hearts and Kim took none. Alex scores 4, Jo 19, Sam 3 and Kim 0 — 26 between them, as every hand should.

Some tables let a player who shoots the moon take 26 off their own score instead. Many also play to 50 for a shorter game. The limit and buttons can be changed to the way your table plays.

What score does Hearts go to?

What happens when you shoot the moon?
